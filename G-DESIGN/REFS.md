## Game references — webdesign-inspiration.com « Jeux Vidéo » (22 sites, gallery opened 2026-11-18)

Lesson of the whole gallery: the sites that hold up carry a **world** (a light, a material, an object, a printed artefact), and the UI lives inside it. None of them looks like a clean app screen.

| Ref | What I saw | Principle kept | Used in |
|-----|-----------|----------------|---------|
| Firewatch | One hue ramp in flat posterised planes, depth by atmosphere (far = lighter), dark silhouette foreground. | Posterised single-hue ramp, atmospheric layering, grain. **Not** its orange palette, its cliff figure or its layout. | A Crépuscule |
| Knucklebon.es · Rustic Citrus | A real material is the stage (worn wood), hand-made type on top. | Material first: the game sits on a physical surface you could touch. | B Laiton |
| Meow Mart · Foot Locker | Printed / signage maximalism: flat bold inks, stickers, hand-made production feel. | The artefact is printed: inks, stickers, stamps, imperfection on purpose. | C Riso |
| Kippo · Infinilearn | A whole illustrated world (isometric town, pixel RPG); the UI panel is framed inside the scene. | The UI is a thing *in* the world, not a layer over it. | all three |

Ignored: Blizzard, Den of Wolves, makemeplay, Kuuasema, TapNation, Gametimer, Cryptic Cup (marketing templates or product photography — exactly the generic look to avoid).

## V1 references (kept for history)

Both references were opened in a browser on 2026-11-18 (home page, first screen and one scroll). Only **principles** are taken: no layout, text, asset, font or brand.

## Dominic Burt — dominicburt.com

What I saw: a warm off-white field, one block of very large neo-grotesk display type set tight (negative tracking, leading close to 1), and almost nothing else. Two or three tiny secondary lines are hung on a grid column far from the headline; the only ornament is a thin arrow glyph in a corner. The page below the fold was still loading (blank), so nothing is taken from it.

Kept:
- **Scale contrast as composition**: one giant element, everything else small and quiet. In Chronomètre the giant element is the number of seconds.
- **Controlled emptiness**: the void is not leftover space, it carries the layout. Fits our motif "the void is the clock".
- **Tight display tracking** with small, loose-ish secondary labels.

## Flow Computing — flow-computing.com

What I saw: a light grey/white field with one large, soft, matte white 3D form lit from above-left (diffuse shadow, no hard edges) behind a black headline whose last line drops to mid-grey. One single solid black vertical tab/button is the only filled control; everything else is text links.

Kept:
- **Soft white relief**: depth from diffuse light on white, not from colour or borders. Used for the button plinth in direction 01.
- **One filled action**: a single black control on the whole screen. Chronomètre has exactly one gesture, so exactly one filled button.
- **Grey as a second voice**: secondary information in the same type, only greyed — no second accent colour.

## Not taken

Any of their layouts, copy, imagery, logos or typefaces. Our type is a system font stack (no licensed font, no network).
