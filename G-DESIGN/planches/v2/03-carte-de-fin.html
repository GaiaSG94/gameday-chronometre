Role

The official result ticket of the heat. Your time printed big, the gap stamped, your streak counted in printed squares, where you sit in today's field — and the one action: comment your time.

The comment line is pre-written and printed on the card, so posting takes one tap and every comment in the thread reads the same way: time, heat, gap. That is what makes the thread fun to scroll.

Style — strict continuity

Same masthead and stub. Pink = you (time, stamp, streak, quote), blue = the race (target, rules, field). Streak squares are halftone tiles; one empty outlined tile = a missed day. The sticker changes job: COMMENT.
