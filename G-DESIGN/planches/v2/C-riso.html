The three states

World

Every day a new race poster comes off the press. Two inks, pink and blue, never quite aligned; where they overlap they turn violet. The heat number, the finish line on the lanes, a sticker to hit.

While you count, the poster goes back to blank paper with only the printer's marks. Then your time is stamped on it — a print you'd want to share.
