World

Every day is one sunset. The target second is the instant the sun touches the sea. You start, night falls, you stop when you feel it — and the sun comes back exactly where your second put it: still hanging (early) or already half-drowned (late).

The gap is not a number first, it is a picture people will describe in the comments.
