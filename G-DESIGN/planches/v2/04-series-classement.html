Role

Why you come back tomorrow. The whole field printed as a halftone histogram — where 3,214 people stopped around the blue finish line — with your bar in pink. The five closest runners of the day, then you. And your streak as a row of printed days.

The histogram is the conversation starter: today's field leaned early, everyone can see it.

Style — strict continuity

Bars are real halftone in blue; your bar overprints pink. Finish line = the same dashed blue as the stub perforation. Ranks in pink only for the top and for you. Streak tiles: dot size = how close you were that day; outlined tile = missed day.

Demo data

Field drawn from a normal curve centred on the median (6.84 s, i.e. 0.46 s early). User names and ranks are placeholders. You: rank 836 of 3,214 (closer than 74 %).
