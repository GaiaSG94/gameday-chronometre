World

A stopwatch someone took the hands out of. It sits on a jeweller's green felt with a paper tag tied to the crown. You press the real crown to start, press it again to stop — and only then does a single red hand swing in to show where your second fell against the red mark.

An object you want to touch: brass, enamel, glass, paper, ink. Every day a new tag.
